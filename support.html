Need help with DON’T.?

DON’T. is a no-contact tracker designed to help you pause before texting, track your progress, check in daily, and keep private thoughts on your device.

Common questions

How do I reset my streak?

If you select YES during your daily check-in and confirm it, your streak restarts from Day 0.

Are my private messages uploaded anywhere?

No. Messages you choose to keep privately are stored locally on your device.

Messages you choose to Burn are not saved to the App’s persistent storage.

Do I need an account?

No. DON’T. does not currently require an account.

How do reminders work?

DON’T. can send optional local notifications for your daily check-in.

You can change or disable notifications at any time in the App or in your iPhone Settings.

How do I restore DON’T+?

Open DON’T., go to the DON’T+ section, and select Restore Purchases.

Purchases are managed through your Apple ID and the App Store.

I purchased DON’T+ but it is not active

First, make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID that was used for the purchase.

Then select Restore Purchases inside DON’T.

If access is still not restored, confirm that the subscription is active under your Apple ID subscription settings, then try Restore Purchases again.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple.

On your iPhone:
Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions

Select DON’T. and manage or cancel your subscription from there.

How do I delete my data?

Most DON’T. data is stored locally on your device.

You can delete individual content where the App provides a delete option.

Removing DON’T. from your device will also remove locally stored App data.

Purchase records managed by Apple may remain according to Apple’s policies.

Troubleshooting

If something is not working as expected, try closing and reopening DON’T., confirm that your iPhone is running a supported iOS version, and make sure the App is up to date.

For DON’T+ purchase issues, use Restore Purchases from the DON’T+ screen and confirm that the subscription is active in your Apple ID subscription settings.

For privacy-related information, see the DON’T. Privacy Policy below.
