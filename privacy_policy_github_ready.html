DON’T.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 5, 2026

DON’T. (“the App”) is designed with privacy in mind.

The App helps users maintain no contact, track their progress, pause before sending a message, complete daily check-ins, and keep private thoughts on their device.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored or processed when you use the App.

1. Information stored on your device

Most information created inside DON’T. is stored locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Your no-contact start date
  • Your streak and progress
  • Your selected goal
  • Daily check-ins
  • Messages you choose to keep privately
  • Remember Why entries
  • Emergency pause activity
  • App preferences
  • Reminder and notification preferences

DON’T. does not currently require an account and does not operate a server containing your journal entries, personal reasons, check-ins, or no-contact history.

2. Private messages and Burn It

Messages written inside the App remain on your device unless otherwise stated.

When you choose Keep Privately, the message is stored locally on your device.

When you choose Burn It, DON’T. is designed not to save the content of that message to the App’s persistent storage.

Burned message content is not sent to DON’T. or RevenueCat.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

DON’T. may offer optional in-app purchases and auto-renewable subscriptions through Apple.

Payments are processed by Apple. DON’T. does not receive or store your payment card or banking information.

The App uses RevenueCat to manage purchases, subscription status, purchase restoration, and access to DON’T+.

RevenueCat may process information required to provide these services, including purchase history, subscription status, transaction information, an anonymous App User ID, and limited technical information related to the device and App.

DON’T. currently uses anonymous RevenueCat user identifiers and does not associate RevenueCat purchase information with a DON’T. account, name, email address, or phone number.

Private journal content, check-ins, Remember Why entries, and unsent message text are not sent to RevenueCat.

4. Notifications

DON’T. may ask for permission to send optional local notifications, such as daily check-in reminders.

These notifications are generated locally on the device.

The App does not use notification content to display private journal entries, personal reasons, or other sensitive written content on the lock screen.

Notifications can be disabled at any time in the App or in iPhone Settings.

5. Advertising and tracking

DON’T. does not display third-party advertising.

DON’T. does not sell personal data.

DON’T. does not use personal data for cross-app advertising tracking.

The App does not use advertising identifiers for behavioral advertising.

6. Analytics

DON’T. does not use general-purpose third-party analytics services to analyze journal entries, personal reasons, unsent messages, or daily check-ins.

RevenueCat processes purchase-related information for subscription functionality and purchase-related analytics.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by DON’T. remains on your device until it is deleted through the App where deletion is available, or until the App and its local data are removed from your device.

Purchase and subscription records processed by Apple and RevenueCat may be retained according to their respective legal obligations and privacy policies.

8. Data sharing

DON’T. does not sell personal information.

Information is processed by third-party service providers only when required to provide specific App functionality, such as Apple for in-app purchases and RevenueCat for subscription management.

DON’T. does not share private journal entries, personal reasons, check-ins, or unsent message content with advertisers.

9. Security

DON’T. is designed to limit the collection of personal information and keep sensitive personal content stored locally whenever possible.

No device, software, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are encouraged to protect their device using a passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, or other available security measures.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to personal information processed by service providers used by DON’T., including rights of access, correction, deletion, or restriction.

Because DON’T. does not currently maintain user accounts or a remote database containing private App content, most personal App data can only be accessed directly from the user’s device.

11. Children

DON’T. is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

The App does not require users to provide a name, email address, phone number, or other direct personal identifier in order to use its core features.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as DON’T. evolves.

If the App begins using new services or processing additional information, this policy will be updated accordingly.

The latest version will always be available on this page.

13. Support and privacy questions

For support or privacy-related questions, please use the public DON’T. support page.

Support information, troubleshooting guidance, subscription help, and privacy-related information are available on the DON’T. support page.
