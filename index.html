DON’T.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

DON’T. (“the App”) is designed with privacy in mind.

The core purpose of DON’T. is to help users maintain no contact, track their progress, pause before sending a message, and privately write thoughts they do not want to send.

This Privacy Policy explains what information is stored or processed when you use the App.

1. Personal data stored on your device

Most information created inside DON’T. is stored locally on your device.

This may include:

  • Your no-contact start date
  • Your streak and progress
  • Your selected goal
  • Daily check-ins
  • Messages you choose to keep privately
  • Personal “Remember Why” reasons
  • Emergency pause activity
  • App preferences
  • Reminder and notification preferences

This information is not uploaded to a DON’T. server.

DON’T. currently does not require an account and does not operate a server containing your journal entries, personal reasons, check-ins, or no-contact history.

2. Private messages and “Burn It”

Messages written inside the App remain on your device unless otherwise stated.

When you choose “Keep Privately”, the message is stored locally on your device.

When you choose “Burn It”, DON’T. is designed not to save the content of that message to the App's persistent storage.

Burned message content is not sent to us.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

DON’T. may offer optional in-app purchases and subscriptions through Apple.

Payments are processed by Apple. DON’T. does not receive or store your payment card or banking information.

The App uses RevenueCat to manage purchases, subscriptions, restore purchases, and premium access.

RevenueCat may process information necessary to provide these services, including purchase history, subscription status, transaction information, an anonymous App User ID, and limited technical information related to the device and App.

DON’T. currently uses anonymous RevenueCat user identifiers and does not associate RevenueCat purchase information with a DON’T. account, email address, name, or phone number.

Purchase information may also be used by RevenueCat to provide subscription-related analytics and functionality.

4. Notifications

DON’T. may ask for permission to send notifications.

Notifications are optional.

The App currently uses local notifications for reminders such as daily check-ins.

DON’T. does not use notification content to display your private journal entries, personal reasons, or other sensitive content on your lock screen.

You can disable notifications at any time through your device settings.

5. Advertising and tracking

DON’T. does not display third-party advertising.

DON’T. does not sell your personal data.

DON’T. does not use your personal data for cross-app advertising tracking.

The App currently does not use advertising identifiers for advertising or behavioral profiling.

6. Analytics

DON’T. does not currently use general-purpose third-party analytics services to analyze your journal entries, personal reasons, messages, or check-ins.

RevenueCat processes purchase-related information in order to provide subscription functionality and purchase analytics.

Private content written in DON’T. is not sent to RevenueCat.

7. Data retention and deletion

Information stored locally by DON’T. remains on your device until it is deleted through the App where deletion is available, or until the App and its local data are removed from your device.

Purchase and subscription records processed by Apple and RevenueCat may be retained according to their respective legal obligations and privacy policies.

8. Data sharing

DON’T. does not sell your personal information.

Information is only processed by third-party service providers when required to provide specific App functionality, such as Apple for in-app purchases and RevenueCat for subscription management.

We do not share your private journal entries, personal reasons, check-ins, or unsent message content with advertisers.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to limit the collection of personal information.

DON’T. has been designed so that sensitive personal content is stored locally whenever possible.

However, no device, software, or storage method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

We recommend protecting your device with a passcode, Face ID, Touch ID, or other available security measures.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights relating to personal information processed by service providers used by DON’T., including rights of access, correction, deletion, or restriction.

Because DON’T. does not currently maintain user accounts or a remote database containing your private App content, much of your personal App data can only be accessed directly from your device.

For privacy-related questions or requests concerning DON’T., you can contact us using the email address below.

11. Children

DON’T. is not designed to intentionally collect personal information from children.

The App does not require users to provide a name, email address, phone number, or other direct personal identifier in order to use its core features.

12. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as DON’T. evolves.

If the App begins using new services or collecting additional information, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

The latest version will always be available at this page.
